# MangaBrain privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

MangaBrain is an Android app for a recommendation server that you run yourself
(the open-source MangaBrain server). The app does nothing until you enter the
address of your own server. This policy describes what the app does with
information. It does not cover the server, which you or the person who runs it
operates, or the third-party services named below, which have their own
policies.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account with us, and we run no server that the app talks to.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone.
- It does not read your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or
  microphone. The only permissions it asks for are internet access and network
  state.

## What the app stores on your device

Kept in the app's private storage, removed when you uninstall it, and not
included in device backups:

- the address of your MangaBrain server and, if your server uses one, its
  access token;
- the profile you chose on that server;
- your filters, weights and display settings (for example the adult-content
  switch and the title language), and the titles in your mix.

## What the app sends to your server

Only to the server address you entered:

- what you search for, describe or open, and the filters you have set;
- the profile you chose and, if set, the access token;
- the account names and tokens you enter in Settings for AniList,
  MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Yamtrack and Hardcover, which your server uses to fetch
  your lists from those services so it can leave out what you have already
  seen.

Your server stores the account details and synced lists. Whoever runs that
server controls that information; you can clear it in Settings or by removing
your profile there.

## Third parties the app contacts directly

To show cover images, the app loads them straight from the services that host
them: AniList (s4.anilist.co), The Movie Database (image.tmdb.org), TVmaze
(static.tvmaze.com) and Open Library (covers.openlibrary.org). Like any web
request, these services receive your device's IP address and a request for the
image. No other information is sent to them by the app.

## Adult content

Adult titles are hidden unless you turn on the adult-content switch in Filters.
The switch is stored only on your device and sent to your server with your
searches.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Deleting your information

- On your device: uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android Settings.
- On your server: clear the account fields in the app's Settings, or remove
  your profile under Settings > Profile. The server's operator can also delete
  it.
